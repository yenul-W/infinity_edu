Method reminder

\[\text{Maximum flow} = \text{Capacity of the minimum cut}\]

Count only edges crossing forward (source side → sink side). Backward edges count as zero. Edges with both endpoints on the same side are not counted at all.

Upper bounds to check first: total capacity out of \(S\), and total capacity into \(T\). The maximum flow cannot exceed either.

Upgrades: only edges in a minimum cut matter. Widening any other edge changes nothing, and widening a bottleneck helps only until the next-smallest cut binds.
