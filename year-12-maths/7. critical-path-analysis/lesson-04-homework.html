Method reminder

\[\text{EST} = \max(\text{previous EST} + \text{duration}) \quad \text{(forward)}\] \[\text{LST} = \min(\text{next LST} - \text{duration}) \quad \text{(backward)}\] \[\text{Float} = \text{LST}_{\text{end}} - \text{EST}_{\text{start}} - \text{duration}\]

Checks: the LST at the start vertex must be 0; the EST at the finish vertex is the minimum completion time; float is never negative.

Zero float = critical. Shortening a non-critical activity changes nothing. Shortening a critical one helps only until the next-longest path takes over.
