How to use this set

Before you start

Do not reveal a solution until you have made a genuine attempt on paper. When you do reveal it, compare your method as well as your answer — most marks in the HSC are for working, not the final number.

Mark yourself honestly with the ✓ / ✗ buttons. The tally at the top of each part shows how you are tracking, and the summary at the end of each part tells you whether to move on or go back to the theory.
