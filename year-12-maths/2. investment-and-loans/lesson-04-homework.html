How to use this set

Before you start

Keep your calculator on hand and write every step. Financial questions carry method marks for identifying the correct formula and substituting properly — a bare number earns very little even when it is right.

Watch the two recurring traps: converting rates and periods for the compounding frequency, and reading whether a percentage applies to the original value (straight-line) or the current value (declining balance, compound interest).
