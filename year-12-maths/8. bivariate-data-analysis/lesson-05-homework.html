Reference

Quick reference

\(|r|\)Strength
0.9 – 1.0Very strong
0.7 – 0.9Strong
0.5 – 0.7Moderate
0.3 – 0.5Weak
0 – 0.3Very weak or none

Every description needs: form (linear / non-linear), direction (positive / negative), strength — all stated in context.

Every prediction needs: the substitution, and a statement of whether it is interpolation (reliable) or extrapolation (unreliable).

\(R^2 \to r\): take the square root, then read the sign from the gradient.
