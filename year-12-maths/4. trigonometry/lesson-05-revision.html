Reference

Formula sheet

Right-angled triangles only

\[\sin \theta = \frac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}} \qquad \cos \theta = \frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}} \qquad \tan \theta = \frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}\]

Inverses \(\sin^{-1}\), \(\cos^{-1}\), \(\tan^{-1}\) find the angle. The hypotenuse is always opposite the right angle.

Sine rule — needs a matching side–angle pair

\[\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C} \quad \text{(finding a side)}\] \[\frac{\sin A}{a} = \frac{\sin B}{b} = \frac{\sin C}{c} \quad \text{(finding an angle)}\]

Put the unknown on top. If told the angle is obtuse, take \(180°\) minus the calculator's answer.

Cosine rule — no matching pair needed

\[c^{2} = a^{2} + b^{2} - 2ab\cos C \quad \text{(SAS → find a side)}\] \[\cos C = \frac{a^{2} + b^{2} - c^{2}}{2ab} \quad \text{(SSS → find an angle)}\]

A negative cosine gives an obtuse angle automatically — no adjustment needed. Remember the square root when finding a side.

Area and angle measure

\[A = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C \qquad\qquad 1° = 60' \qquad 1' = 60''\]

\(C\) must be the angle between sides \(a\) and \(b\). Decimal → minutes: multiply by 60. Minutes → decimal: divide by 60.

Facts that check your work

  • Angles of a triangle sum to \(180°\).
  • The longest side is opposite the largest angle.
  • \(\sin\theta = \sin(180° - \theta)\), so inverse sine is ambiguous.
  • \(\sin\theta \le 1\) always — a ratio above 1 means the sides are mixed up.

The one-question test

Is there a matching pair — a side and the angle directly opposite it? Yes → sine rule. No → cosine rule. A right angle beats both.

Which tool?

Type sine, cosine or soh cah toa.

Bearings reference

Two systems

True bearings — clockwise from north, three digits: \(000°\) to \(359°\).
Compass bearings — N or S, an acute angle, then E or W: \(N35°E\), \(S62°W\).

QuadrantTrue bearing rangeCompass formConvert
North-east\(000°\)–\(090°\)\(N\theta E\)\(\theta = \) bearing
South-east\(090°\)–\(180°\)\(S\theta E\)\(\theta = 180° - \) bearing
South-west\(180°\)–\(270°\)\(S\theta W\)\(\theta = \) bearing \(- 180°\)
North-west\(270°\)–\(360°\)\(N\theta W\)\(\theta = 360° - \) bearing

Back bearings and interior angles

Back bearing: add 180° if the bearing is under 180°, subtract 180° if it is 180° or more.

Interior angle at a turning point: take the back bearing of the incoming leg, then find the difference from the outgoing bearing. The raw difference between the two bearings is the angle turned through, not the interior angle.

Exam technique

Mistakes that cost marks

MistakeFix
Calculator in radian mode Check for DEG before the first calculation. Every answer will be wrong otherwise.
Labelling opposite/adjacent from the wrong angle Label the diagram relative to the angle in the question, every time.
Multiplying when the unknown is on the bottom \(\sin\theta = \frac{k}{x}\) means \(x = \frac{k}{\sin\theta}\) — divide.
Forgetting the square root in the cosine rule The rule gives \(c^{2}\). Take the root.
Treating \(\cos\) of an obtuse angle as positive It is negative, and subtracting it adds to \(c^{2}\).
Accepting the acute answer when told the angle is obtuse Take \(180°\) minus the calculator's value.
Using the area formula with a non-included angle \(C\) must sit between \(a\) and \(b\). Find the right angle first if necessary.
Forgetting to add the observer's eye height The triangle starts at eye level, not the ground.
Putting the depression angle at the top of the triangle Transfer it to the bottom, where it becomes the angle of elevation.
Subtracting raw bearings for the interior angle Take the back bearing of the incoming leg first.
Writing a true bearing as \(45°\) True bearings take three digits: \(045°\).
Rounding mid-calculation Keep full precision in the calculator; round only the final answer.
