Formula sheet

\[\sin \theta = \frac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}} \qquad \cos \theta = \frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}} \qquad \tan \theta = \frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}\] \[\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C} \qquad \frac{\sin A}{a} = \frac{\sin B}{b} = \frac{\sin C}{c}\] \[c^{2} = a^{2} + b^{2} - 2ab\cos C \qquad \cos C = \frac{a^{2} + b^{2} - c^{2}}{2ab}\] \[A = \frac{1}{2}ab\sin C \qquad 1° = 60'\]

Check your calculator is in degree mode before you start. Remember that \(\sin\theta = \sin(180° - \theta)\), so inverse sine returns only the acute solution.
