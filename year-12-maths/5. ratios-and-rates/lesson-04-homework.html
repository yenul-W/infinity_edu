Conversions: \(1\) m³ \(= 1000\) L \(= 1\) kL; \(1\) ha \(= 10\,000\) m²; \(1\) mm \(= 0.001\) m; \(100\,000\) cm \(= 1\) km. To convert km/h to m/s, divide by 3.6.
