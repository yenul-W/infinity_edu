Formula reminder

\[P(E) = \frac{\text{favourable outcomes}}{\text{total outcomes}} \qquad P(\text{not } E) = 1 - P(E)\] \[P(A \text{ and } B) = P(A) \times P(B) \quad \text{(independent)}\] \[\text{Relative frequency} = \frac{\text{occurrences}}{\text{trials}} \qquad \text{Expected frequency} = np\]

Trees: multiply along a path ("and"), add between paths ("or"). All path probabilities must sum to 1.

Without replacement: both the favourable count and the total drop by one.

"At least one": use the complement — \(1 - P(\text{none})\).
