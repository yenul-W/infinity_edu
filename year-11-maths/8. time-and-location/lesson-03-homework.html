Question 1 Lesson 1

A ship's position is recorded as \(42°\text{S}, 8°\text{W}\). What does this tell us?

A 42° north of the equator, 8° east of the Prime Meridian
B 42° south of the equator, 8° west of the Prime Meridian
C 8° south of the equator, 42° west of the Prime Meridian
D 42° south of the equator, 8° east of the Prime Meridian
Answer: B
The latitude 42°S places it 42° south of the equator; the longitude 8°W places it 8° west of the Prime Meridian.

Question 2 Lesson 1

Which line of reference is used to measure longitude?

A The equator
B The Tropic of Capricorn
C The Prime Meridian
D The International Date Line
Answer: C — The Prime Meridian
Longitude is measured east or west of the Prime Meridian (0°), which passes through Greenwich, England.

Question 3 Lesson 1

Two cities are located at \(5°\text{W}\) and \(40°\text{E}\). What is the time difference between them?

A 2 hours
B 3 hours
C 4 hours
D 5 hours
Answer: B — 3 hours
Difference in longitude \(= 40° - (-5°) = 45°\). Time difference \(= 45 \div 15 = 3\) hours.

Question 4 Lesson 1

City M is at \(90°\text{E}\) and City N is at \(30°\text{E}\). Which statement is correct?

A City M is ahead by 4 hours
B City N is ahead by 4 hours
C City M is ahead by 2 hours
D City N is ahead by 2 hours
Answer: A — City M is ahead by 4 hours
Difference in longitude \(= 90° - 30° = 60°\). Time difference \(= 60 \div 15 = 4\) hours. City M is further east, so it is ahead.

Question 5 Lesson 2

How many seconds are there in 3.5 minutes?

A 180
B 200
C 210
D 350
Answer: C — 210
\(3.5 \times 60 = 210\) seconds.

Question 6 Lesson 2

Convert 8:05 pm to 24-hour time.

A 0805
B 1805
C 2005
D 2105
Answer: C — 2005
Add 12 to the pm hour: \(8 + 12 = 20\), so 8:05 pm \(= 2005\).

Question 7 Lesson 2

Convert 0730 (24-hour time) to 12-hour time.

A 7:30 am
B 7:30 pm
C 5:30 am
D 9:30 am
Answer: A — 7:30 am
The hour is less than 12, so 0730 is simply 7:30 am.

Question 8 Lesson 2

A ferry departs at 11:40 am and arrives at 2:15 pm. How long is the trip?

A 2 hours 15 minutes
B 2 hours 35 minutes
C 3 hours 35 minutes
D 1 hour 35 minutes
Answer: B — 2 hours 35 minutes
11:40 am to 2:00 pm is 2 hours 20 minutes; plus 15 more minutes to 2:15 pm \(= 2\) hours 35 minutes.

Question 9 Lesson 2

Which of these does NOT observe daylight saving?

A New South Wales
B South Australia
C Northern Territory
D Tasmania
Answer: C — Northern Territory
The Northern Territory, along with Queensland and Western Australia, does not observe daylight saving.

Question 10 Lesson 2

It is 11:00 am AEST (UTC+10) in Sydney. What is the UTC time?

A 1:00 am
B 9:00 pm (previous day)
C 1:00 pm
D 9:00 am
Answer: A — 1:00 am
To convert local time to UTC, subtract the offset: \(11\text{:}00\text{ am} - 10\text{h} = 1\text{:}00\text{ am}\) UTC.

\(63° - 18° = 45°\)
(b) What is the time difference between them, in hours? 1
\(45 \div 15 = 3\) hours
(c) Which station is ahead in time? 1
Station
Station B, since \(63°\text{E}\) is further east than \(18°\text{E}\).

Question 12 3 marks Lesson 1

A plane flies from a city at \(100°\text{E}\) to a city at \(145°\text{E}\).

(a) What is the difference in longitude between the two cities? 1
\(145° - 100° = 45°\)
(b) What is the time difference, in hours? 1
\(45 \div 15 = 3\) hours
(c) If it is 6:00 am at the first city, what time is it at the destination? 1
The destination is further east, so it is ahead: \(6\text{:}00\text{ am} + 3\text{h} = 9\text{:}00\text{ am}\).

Question 14 4 marks Lesson 2

A conference call is scheduled for 7:00 pm AEDT (UTC+11) in Sydney, during daylight saving.

(a) What is the UTC time of the call? 1
\(7\text{:}00\text{ pm} - 11\text{h} = 8\text{:}00\text{ am}\) UTC.
(b) Perth is on AWST (UTC+8) and does not observe daylight saving. What local time in Perth corresponds to the call? 2
UTC \(8\text{:}00\text{ am} + 8\text{h} = 4\text{:}00\text{ pm}\) in Perth.
(c) What is the time difference between Sydney (AEDT) and Perth (AWST) during daylight saving? 1
\(11 - 8 = 3\) hours.
