Quick Reference

Key Formulas & Concepts

Lesson 1 — Positions on the Earth's Surface

  • Latitudedistance north/south of the equator (0°)
  • Longitudedistance east/west of the Prime Meridian (0°)
  • Position = (latitude, longitude)e.g. 33.9°S, 151.2°E
  • Time difference (h) = longitude diff ÷ 15east = ahead, west = behind

Lesson 2 — Time and Time Differences

  • 1 min = 60 s, 1 h = 60 min = 3600 smultiply to go smaller, divide to go larger
  • pm → 24h: add 1212 pm stays 1200; am hour stays the same (12 am = 0000)
  • AEST = UTC+10, ACST = UTC+9:30, AWST = UTC+8daylight saving adds 1 hour (not QLD, NT, WA)
  • Local → UTC: subtract offsetUTC → local: add offset

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ! Mixing up the N/S and E/W direction letters — they change which hemisphere/side of the Prime Meridian a point is on.
  • ! Forgetting that east means ahead in time and west means behind.
  • ! Off-by-12 errors converting pm to 24-hour time — remember 12 pm stays 1200, not 2400.
  • ! Adding instead of subtracting (or vice versa) when converting between local time and UTC.
  • ! Forgetting that Queensland, the Northern Territory and Western Australia do not observe daylight saving.

Question 1 · Latitude & Longitude

A location is at 15°N, 60°W. Which statement is correct?

A North of the equator, east of the Prime Meridian
B South of the equator, west of the Prime Meridian
C North of the equator, west of the Prime Meridian
D South of the equator, east of the Prime Meridian
Answer: C
15°N places it north of the equator; 60°W places it west of the Prime Meridian.

Question 2 · Longitude & Time

Two cities differ by 60° of longitude. What is the time difference between them?

A 2 hours
B 4 hours
C 6 hours
D 8 hours
Answer: B — 4 hours
Time difference = 60 ÷ 15 = 4 hours.

Question 3 · Time Conversion

Convert 6:15 pm to 24-hour time.

A 0615
B 1815
C 1715
D 0715
Answer: B — 1815
Add 12 to the pm hour: 6 + 12 = 18, so 6:15 pm = 1815.

Question 4 · Time Conversion

Convert 2330 (24-hour time) to 12-hour time.

A 11:30 am
B 1:30 pm
C 11:30 pm
D 9:30 pm
Answer: C — 11:30 pm
23 − 12 = 11, and since the hour was 13 or greater it is pm: 2330 = 11:30 pm.

Question 5 · Elapsed Time

A flight departs at 10:15 am and lands at 3:50 pm. How long is the flight?

A 5 hours 35 minutes
B 4 hours 35 minutes
C 5 hours 25 minutes
D 6 hours 5 minutes
Answer: A — 5 hours 35 minutes
10:15 am to 3:00 pm is 4 h 45 min; plus 50 more minutes = 5 h 35 min.

Question 6 · Time Zones

Which describes Queensland's time zone arrangement?

A AEST, observes daylight saving
B AEST, does not observe daylight saving
C ACST, observes daylight saving
D AWST, does not observe daylight saving
Answer: B
Queensland is in the AEST zone (UTC+10) but does not observe daylight saving.

Question 7 · Time Zones

It is 4:00 pm AEDT in Sydney (daylight saving in effect). What is the time in Queensland, which stays on AEST?

A 3:00 pm
B 5:00 pm
C 4:00 pm
D 2:00 pm
Answer: A — 3:00 pm
AEDT is UTC+11, AEST is UTC+10, a 1-hour difference. Queensland is behind: 4:00 pm − 1 h = 3:00 pm.

Question 8 · UTC

Local time is 9:00 am AEST (UTC+10). What is the UTC time?

A 11:00 pm (previous day)
B 7:00 pm (same day)
C 11:00 am
D 7:00 am
Answer: A — 11:00 pm (previous day)
Subtract the offset: 9:00 am − 10 h = 11:00 pm the previous day.

Question 9 · UTC

UTC time is 14:00. What is the local time in Perth (AWST, UTC+8)?

A 6:00 am
B 10:00 pm
C 10:00 am
D 6:00 pm
Answer: B — 10:00 pm
Add the offset: 14:00 + 8 h = 22:00 = 10:00 pm.

Question 10 · Mixed

A location is at 10°S, 25°E. Which statement is correct?

A South of the equator, east of the Prime Meridian
B North of the equator, west of the Prime Meridian
C South of the equator, west of the Prime Meridian
D North of the equator, east of the Prime Meridian
Answer: A
10°S places it south of the equator; 25°E places it east of the Prime Meridian.
