Key Formulas & Concepts

Lesson 1 — Units & Practicalities

  • Length ×10 per stepmm → cm → m → km
  • Area ×100 per step, volume ×1000square/cube the length factor
  • 1 cm³ = 1 mL, 1 m³ = 1000 Lthe volume/capacity bridge
  • Absolute error = ½ × precision% error = abs. error ÷ measurement × 100

Lesson 2 — Area, Surface Area & Volume

  • \(A = \tfrac{1}{2}bh\) (triangle), \(A = lb\) (rectangle)and \(A=\tfrac{1}{2}h(a+b)\) (trapezium)
  • \(A = \pi r^2\) (circle), \(A = \tfrac{\theta}{360}\pi r^2\) (sector)
  • \(SA = 2\pi r^2+2\pi rh\) (cylinder), \(SA=4\pi r^2\) (sphere)
  • \(V=Ah\) (prism), \(V=\pi r^2h\) (cylinder), \(V=\tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3\) (sphere), \(V=\tfrac{1}{3}Ah\) (cone/pyramid)

Lesson 3 — Trapezoidal Rule

  • \(A \approx \tfrac{h}{2}[d_{f}+d_{l}+2(\text{middle sum})]\)h = fixed perpendicular spacing
  • Middle offsets: weight 2shared between two trapezia
  • Works for area, volume and capacityvolume = area estimate × depth

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ! Converting area with the length factor — e.g. using ×100 instead of ×10 000 when converting m² to cm².
  • ! Forgetting to convert all lengths to the same unit before multiplying (e.g. mixing cm and m in a volume calculation).
  • ! Using the full precision as the absolute error instead of half of it.
  • ! Forgetting to double the middle offsets in the Trapezoidal rule (only the first and last keep weight 1).
  • ! For composite solids, remembering that internal joining faces are not part of the exposed surface area.

Question 1 · Units

How many cubic centimetres are there in a cubic metre?

A 100
B 10 000
C 100 000
D 1 000 000
Answer: D
1 m³ = 100 × 100 × 100 cm = 1 000 000 cm³.

Question 2 · Measurement Error

A person's weight is measured as 79.3 kg. What is the absolute error of this measurement?

A 10 grams
B 50 grams
C 100 grams
D 500 grams
Answer: B
Precision is 0.1 kg. Absolute error = ½ × 0.1 kg = 0.05 kg = 50 g.

Question 3 · Area

A shape consists of a quadrant (radius 8 cm) and a rectangle 16 cm × 8 cm attached along the radius. Which expression gives its area?

A \(\tfrac{\pi \times 8^2}{4} + 16 \times 8\)
B \(\pi \times 8^2 + 16 \times 8\)
C \(\tfrac{\pi \times 16^2}{4} + 8^2\)
D \(\tfrac{\pi \times 4^2}{4} + 8^2\)
Answer: A
Quadrant area = ¼πr², plus the rectangle's area l × b.

Question 4 · Volume

A rectangular prism has a base area of 4.1 m² and a height of 60 cm. What is its volume, in cubic metres?

A 0.246 m³
B 2.46 m³
C 24.6 m³
D 246 m³
Answer: B
Convert height to metres first: 60 cm = 0.6 m. \(V = Ah = 4.1 \times 0.6 = 2.46\) m³.

Question 5 · Trapezoidal Rule

A field is measured using four applications of the Trapezoidal rule with offsets 6, 7, 12, 8, 10 m, spaced h m apart. Which offsets are given weight 2 in the formula?

A All five offsets
B Only the first and last (6 and 10)
C The three middle offsets (7, 12, 8)
D None of the offsets
Answer: C
Every offset except the first and last is shared between two adjoining trapezia, so it is counted twice.

Question 6 · Surface Area

A sphere has a surface area of 452 m². What is its approximate volume?

A 113 m³
B 904 m³
C 9 610 m³
D 24 366 m³
Answer: B
\(452 = 4\pi r^2 \Rightarrow r \approx 6.0\) m. \(V = \tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \approx 904\) m³.

Question 7 · Pythagoras

A right-angled triangle has a vertical side of 6 cm and a hypotenuse of 10 cm, which is also the radius of an attached quadrant. What is that radius?

A 6 cm
B 8 cm
C 10 cm
D 12 cm
Answer: B
\(r = \sqrt{10^2 - 6^2} = \sqrt{64} = 8\) cm.

Question 8 · Scientific Notation

What is 0.002073 expressed in standard form, correct to two significant figures?

A \(2.07 \times 10^{-2}\)
B \(2.1 \times 10^{-2}\)
C \(2.07 \times 10^{-3}\)
D \(2.1 \times 10^{-3}\)
Answer: D
\(0.002073 = 2.073 \times 10^{-3} \approx 2.1 \times 10^{-3}\) (2 sig. figs).

Question 9 · Capacity

During a flood, 1.5 hectares of land was covered by water to a depth of 17 cm. How many kilolitres of water covered the land?

A 2.55 kL
B 2550 kL
C 255 000 kL
D 2 550 000 kL
Answer: B
Area = 1.5 × 10 000 = 15 000 m². Volume = 15 000 × 0.17 = 2550 m³ = 2550 kL.

Question 10 · Mixed

A container is a triangular prism with capacity 12 L and base area 240 cm². What is the distance between its triangular ends?

A 5 cm
B 20 cm
C 25 cm
D 50 cm
Answer: D
12 L = 12 000 cm³. \(h = 12\,000 \div 240 = 50\) cm.
